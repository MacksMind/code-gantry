Decide whether to revise this stage, insert a predecessor stage before it, or stop. A revision keeps the stage's identity, so the report reads as one stage that took two attempts rather than pretending they were different work.

If you revise, `revision_mode` decides the fate of the work already on the branch: `extend` keeps it (right when the scope was merely too narrow), `restart` discards it (right when the approach was wrong).
